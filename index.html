Our Vision, Mission & Chronological History

Core Mission Tenets

The Ilocos Biblical Baptist Institute operates under the calling and commission of God. Our core guidelines shape every curriculum module and spiritual discipline:

  1. 1

    Through God’s grace, to do everything by faith in all we can to lead men and women in giving their hearts and lives to the Lord Jesus Christ and to secure eternal life that He offers.

  2. 2

    To do all we can to minister to the spiritual needs in this world.

  3. 3

    To share God’s Word, the holy Bible, and to carry Christ's Great Commission into all the world.

  4. 4

    To assist the believers including both Pastors and Christian workers alike in their understanding, teaching and preaching of God’s Word.

  5. 5

    To give the Lord Jesus Christ proper honor and place, the pre-eminent place in which the word of God has given Him.

Historical Timeline

11 October 1993

Conceptual Foundation

The conceptual voting for the establishment of the IBBI school was carried out during the monthly Pastor’s prayer meeting held at the Bayubay Baptist Church in San Vicente, Ilocos Sur.

June 1994 – March 1995

Grand Opening & First Academic Year

Classes commenced utilizing the facilities of Taleb Baptist Church in Taleb, Bantay, Ilocos Sur. The school year began with 7 pioneer students, leading to the graduation of its first batch of 3 students in March 1997.

June 2002

Internship Curriculum Expansion

The core training framework was expanded to formally include an additional 1-year practical ministerial Internship training track, bringing the overall curriculum path to 4 years total.

2020 – 2024

Flexible Adaptation Protocols

Under the global restrictions and pandemic lockdowns, IBBI executed flexible adaptation protocols, routing teaching operations effectively via 7 Satellite Churches to sustain training without pause.

OUR FOUNDATION

Doctrinal Statement Summary

The doctrinal anchors that secure our faith, instruction, and ministry practices. We stand firmly on fundamental Baptist doctrines.

We believe in the 66 canonical books of the Old and New Testaments as the inspired, inerrant Word of God. They constitute the absolute and final standard of faith and practice. We interpret Scriptures literally, grammatically, and dispensationally.

KJV Standard Literal Interpretation

We believe in the Triune God: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. They are co-equal in power and glory, eternal, immutable, and co-existent, possessing the same attributes and perfections.

We believe salvation is wholly of grace, independent of human works or merit. It is made possible solely by the substitutionary death and shed blood of our Lord Jesus Christ, received by personal faith, resulting in instantaneous regeneration, and secured eternally in Christ.

We believe in the local church as an autonomous, self-governing body of believers, with congregational government under the headship of Christ. We observe two ordinances: Believer's Baptism by Immersion, serving as a public testimony, and the Lord's Supper, observed as a solemn memorial of His sacrifice.

We believe in strict separation from religious apostasy, compromise, and all worldly practices that dishonor the Lord. In keeping with this biblical standard, we hold a Pro-King James Version Bible position and strictly maintain a Non-Contemporary Christian Music (non-CCM) environment in our services, classroom work, and dormitories.

STUDENT ENVIRONMENT

Campus Life & Spiritual Standards

A typical day at IBBI focuses on spiritual discipline, intense Bible work, character molding, and active local church ministry.

⏱

Daily Devotions & Chapel

Every morning starts with group devotions, followed by standard Chapel services from 9:00 AM to 10:00 AM. Attendance is mandatory for all in-campus students, cultivating a continuous spirit of prayer and worship.

✂

Strict Grooming Guidelines

To foster professional discipline, men wear dress pants and collared shirts with clean-cut hair. Women wear modest dresses or skirts extending well below the knee, maintaining a neat appearance without slacks or short hairstyles.

📱

Mobile Device Regulations

To preserve focus and ensure distraction-free studies, student cellphones are collected on Mondays at 12:00 PM and safely returned on Fridays at 4:00 PM for the weekend break.
